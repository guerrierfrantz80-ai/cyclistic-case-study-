### Business Task
How to convert casual riders to annual members?

### Key Insights (from SQL)
- Casual riders ride 45% longer than members
- Casuals: 68% of rides on weekends (leisure)
- Members: 70% on weekdays 7-9AM (commuters)
- Top 10 casual stations are near tourist attractions

### My 3 Recommendations
1. Weekend Explorer Promo: 20% off membership after 3 weekend rides
2. Tourist to Member: Ads at top 10 casual stations
3. Commuter Benefit: Highlight cost savings for daily use
